I originally made and taught this workshop at the Bellevue College Makerspace I helped setup. The objective was to impart on the participants that all of those cool Arduino projects you see could actually be turned into dedicated electronics circuits they can call their own. This was one of my favorite lessons I learned in my time at the StudentRND Hackerspace I originally got involved with back in 2012, when SRND still had a physical space of it's own.

The premise of the device was simple and was basically a paired down Arduino in it's own. By using an ATTiny85 and a few other simple components I was able to get the bill of materials down low enough that each participant could have their own kit to build and take home at a reasonable cost.
